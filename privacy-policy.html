StyloBuddy Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

StyloBuddy is provided by Talha Hussain. Contact us at th47555@gmail.com about privacy, your data, or this policy.

Information we process

We use Firebase Authentication to create an anonymous account when you start. If you choose Google or Sign in with Apple, we also process the account information those services provide, such as your name and email address, including an Apple private relay address where applicable.

We process photos you choose to capture or upload, clothing descriptions and attributes, wardrobe items, style preferences, styling requests, saved ideas and wear history to provide the app. Camera and photo-library access are used for the features you choose.

When you enable weather suggestions and permit location access, we request low-accuracy location and send coordinates to Open-Meteo to retrieve local weather. Location is used for weather context rather than advertising. You can disable weather access in the app or revoke location permission in iOS Settings.

Firebase Analytics processes app interaction events to help us understand use of the app. Firebase Crashlytics processes crash reports and technical diagnostics to help us find and fix problems. These services may process device, app-instance and technical identifiers.

Apple and RevenueCat process subscription purchases, transaction identifiers and subscription status so we can enable access, restore purchases and manage entitlements. RevenueCat uses your app user identifier to associate purchases with your account. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

AI processing and service providers

StyloBuddy sends selected photos, clothing attributes and styling requests through our Supabase backend to OpenAI for clothing recognition and outfit or shopping suggestions. Information you include in an AI request may be processed by OpenAI. Do not submit sensitive personal information or photos you do not have permission to use.

Firebase stores authentication information and app records in Firestore. Supabase stores uploaded photos and runs backend functions. OpenAI processes AI requests. Open-Meteo supplies weather. Apple and RevenueCat support subscriptions. These providers process information to deliver their services; their own terms, privacy practices and applicable retention requirements also apply.

We use information to operate and secure StyloBuddy, provide and improve its features, support customers, resolve technical problems and meet legal obligations. We do not use an advertising identifier or sell personal information for targeted advertising. Discover links may take you to external stores whose privacy practices apply separately.

Retention and deletion

Account and wardrobe records and uploaded photos are retained while needed to provide your account and its features, until you delete them or your account. You can delete your account from Profile in the app. Account deletion removes the app's wardrobe records, wear history, saved ideas, stored photos and Firebase authentication account. It may require you to sign in again to confirm the request.

Deleting the app alone does not delete your account. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription: manage or cancel it in your Apple account subscription settings. Purchase records, security logs, diagnostics and provider backups may remain where required for legal, security or operational purposes and according to the relevant provider's retention practices. Contact us for assistance with a data request.

Your choices and rights

You can choose whether to link a sign-in provider, upload photos, and enable weather. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal information, object to certain processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a data protection authority. Contact th47555@gmail.com to make a request. We may need to verify your identity.

Providers may process information in countries outside your country of residence. We use access controls and encrypted network connections to protect information, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

StyloBuddy is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate authorization, contact us.

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will publish the current version at this page and provide additional notice where required.
